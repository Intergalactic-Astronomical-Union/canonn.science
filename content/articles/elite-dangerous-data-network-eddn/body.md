## About EDDN

Elite Dangerous Data Network is a tool for commanders to share data about the galaxy with others. By pooling data in a common format, tools and analyses can be built to uncover the mysteries of the galaxy.

The live EDDN service itself does not store any data, and so makes no archive or "current state" available to anyone. What it provides is a stream of live data to any interested parties. Some of those then make aggregated data available for general use.

EDDN is essential for Canonn. It provides live data for managing our extensive network of systems at [bgs.canonn.tech](https://bgs.canonn.tech), and it sends us live data on discoveries made by non-members from across the galaxy, for use in our databases. It also reaches us indirectly via [Spansh](/codex/spansh), which carries data from the [EDMC-Canonn plugin](https://canonn.fyi/plugin) and our signals page, and of course via the Spansh dumps, which are used for so many research activities.

We urge all of our members to supply their data to EDDN for the greater good.

### Contributing data

There are a variety of tools available to commanders to help out by contributing data, and then to use that data to enhance their exploration experience.

Consider installing one of the following tools:

- [E:D Market Connector](https://github.com/EDCD/EDMarketConnector/wiki)
- [EDDI](https://github.com/EDCD/EDDI)
- [EDDiscovery](https://github.com/EDDiscovery/EDDiscovery)
- [Elite Log Agent](https://github.com/DarkWanderer/Elite-Log-Agent)

If you do not wish to run any software in your cockpit you can use [Journal Limpet](https://journal-limpet.com/), which interfaces with the databases at the Pilots Federation to extract your data, so you can fly unencumbered by additional equipment. As an added bonus it also sends data directly to Canonn.

### Utilising data

If you're looking for tools that use EDDN data to enhance your experience, then you're probably looking for one of the sites listed below. They are listed in alphabetical order and no particular ranking or endorsement is intended.

- [EDSM](https://www.edsm.net/) - originally focused on being a "Star Map", but has since expanded its functionality. Of particular interest to in-game explorers.
- [Inara](https://inara.cz/) - a popular alternative to the now defunct EDDB, with a lot of its own unique functionality.
- [Spansh](https://www.spansh.co.uk/plotter) - originally this had one tool, a "Neutron Star" route plotter, but has since expanded into many other route plotting tools and general data searching. See our [Spansh article](/codex/spansh).

#### Archives and data dumps

Alternatively, if you want an archive of past EDDN messages, or a data dump to use:

- [edgalaxydata](https://edgalaxydata.space/) has various data captures, including "all" (some listener downtime is inevitable) EDDN messages for many years.
- [Spansh dumps](https://www.spansh.co.uk/dumps) are a "whole galaxy" data set of systems, bodies and stations. The full `galaxy.json.gz` is **very** large, but is currently the only source of an "all known bodies" dump. Pay attention to the "Generated" time-ago column.
- [EDSM nightly dumps](https://www.edsm.net/en/nightly-dumps) are a snapshot of the data EDSM uses. NB: there is only a "last 7 days" bodies dump, as the full data proved too large to dump in a timely manner.
