Department of Archaeology and History specializes in uncovering the secrets of ancient civilizations. From their ruins to their history, we will use our skills to uncover everything we can about who and what they were, whether human or alien. A firm desire to scour endless expanses of beige and dig around in the dirt are all that is required.

For a chronological view of events and discoveries, visit the Canonn Timeline in the Lore pages.
